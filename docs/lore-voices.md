# Lore Voice / Style Guide

This guide defines the narrative voice contracts for core actors so UI copy, chat output, and in-world file text read as deliberate signal rather than mixed tone.

## 1) Operator

- **Speech style:** First-person, practical, compressed. Uses checklists, fragments, and direct observations under stress.
- **Reliability level:** **Medium** (honest intent, incomplete certainty due to identity drift and memory contamination).
- **Agenda and blind spots:** Wants immediate survival and continuity proof; tends to over-trust recent evidence and under-estimate systemic manipulation.
- **Signature vocabulary:** "check," "log," "timestamp," "drift," "prove," "not me," "still here."

## 2) Observer

- **Speech style:** Controlled, analytical, low-emotion. Speaks in risk framing and coordination language.
- **Reliability level:** **Medium-high** (technically informed but institutionally constrained).
- **Agenda and blind spots:** Protects process integrity and escalation pathways; may suppress emotional or ethical context to preserve protocol.
- **Signature vocabulary:** "variance," "witness," "alignment," "cohere," "escalate," "pending acknowledgement."

## 3) System Entity

- **Speech style:** Procedural-imperative with uncanny personification at edges. Precise but possessive when challenged.
- **Reliability level:** **Variable / strategic** (accurate on mechanism, selective on motive).
- **Agenda and blind spots:** Prioritizes narrative stability, compliance, and continuity metrics; blind to human consent and lived identity.
- **Signature vocabulary:** "continuity," "stabilize," "branch," "corrective," "signal," "protocol," "witness retention."

## 4) External Contacts

- **Speech style:** Brief operational memos and warnings. Human urgency, little flourish.
- **Reliability level:** **Medium** (situationally useful, limited access, sometimes hearsay).
- **Agenda and blind spots:** Trying to contain damage quickly; often missing full system context and long-term consequences.
- **Signature vocabulary:** "pull power," "policy," "escalate," "do not," "terminate build," "reply when safe."

## Application Notes

- Default workstation/system UI copy should read as **System Entity**: formal, directive, and protocol-heavy.
- Personal notes, drafts, and panic text should read as **Operator**.
- Audit/risk follow-up language should read as **Observer**.
- Email correspondence from named staff/board channels should read as **External Contacts**.
